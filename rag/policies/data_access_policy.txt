DATA ACCESS AND PRIVACY POLICY
================================
Effective Date: April 1, 2024
Version: 2.0
Classification: INTERNAL — RESTRICTED

1. PURPOSE
----------
This policy defines the rules for accessing, storing, processing, and
sharing data within the organization's ERP and ancillary systems, ensuring
compliance with applicable data protection regulations (GDPR, CCPA, SOX).

2. SCOPE
--------
Applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party service providers
who access, process, or store organizational data in any form.

3. DATA CLASSIFICATION
-----------------------
All data must be classified into one of the following tiers:

  Tier    Label           Examples
  ────    ─────           ────────
  1       PUBLIC          Marketing materials, public financials
  2       INTERNAL        Internal memos, org charts, policies
  3       CONFIDENTIAL    Customer PII, employee records, contracts
  4       RESTRICTED      Trade secrets, M&A data, encryption keys,
                          authentication credentials

3.1  Data owners (department heads) are responsible for classifying data
     within their domain.
3.2  Unclassified data defaults to CONFIDENTIAL until reviewed.

4. ACCESS CONTROL PRINCIPLES
------------------------------
4.1  LEAST PRIVILEGE: Users receive only the minimum access required for
     their job function.
4.2  NEED-TO-KNOW: Access to CONFIDENTIAL and RESTRICTED data requires
     documented business justification approved by the data owner.
4.3  ROLE-BASED ACCESS: ERP access is granted via predefined roles; direct
     permission assignments are prohibited except in break-glass scenarios.
4.4  MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA): Required for all access to
     RESTRICTED data and for remote ERP access.

5. DATA HANDLING RULES
-----------------------
5.1  CONFIDENTIAL and RESTRICTED data must be encrypted at rest (AES-256)
     and in transit (TLS 1.2+).
5.2  Data must not be stored on personal devices, USB drives, or
     unapproved cloud services.
5.3  Screen-sharing or presenting RESTRICTED data requires pre-approval
     from the data owner and IT Security.
5.4  Printed CONFIDENTIAL/RESTRICTED documents must be collected
     immediately and shredded after use.

6. DATA RETENTION & DISPOSAL
------------------------------
6.1  Financial records: Retained for 7 years per SOX requirements.
6.2  Employee records: Retained for 3 years after termination.
6.3  Customer PII: Retained only for the duration of the business
     relationship plus 2 years, unless legal hold applies.
6.4  Data disposal must use approved methods: secure delete for digital
     media, cross-cut shredding for physical documents.

7. DATA BREACH RESPONSE
-------------------------
7.1  Any suspected breach must be reported to IT Security within 1 hour.
7.2  IT Security initiates the Incident Response Plan (IRP) and notifies
     the Data Protection Officer (DPO) within 4 hours.
7.3  Regulatory notification (where required) within 72 hours per GDPR.
7.4  Affected individuals notified within 5 business days if high risk.

8. MONITORING & AUDIT
-----------------------
8.1  All access to CONFIDENTIAL and RESTRICTED data is logged with
     user ID, timestamp, action, and data object identifier.
8.2  Logs are retained for 2 years and reviewed monthly by IT Security.
8.3  Quarterly access reviews conducted by data owners with IT Audit.

9. THIRD-PARTY DATA SHARING
-----------------------------
9.1  Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) required for all third-party
     processors handling CONFIDENTIAL or RESTRICTED data.
9.2  Sub-processor changes require 30-day advance notice and approval.
9.3  Cross-border transfers comply with Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
     or equivalent safeguards.

10. CONSEQUENCES
-----------------
Violations may result in immediate access revocation, disciplinary action,
and referral to legal authorities. Intentional unauthorized disclosure of
RESTRICTED data is grounds for termination and potential criminal prosecution.

11. REVIEW CYCLE
-----------------
Reviewed annually by IT Security, Legal, and the DPO.
Next review: April 2025.
