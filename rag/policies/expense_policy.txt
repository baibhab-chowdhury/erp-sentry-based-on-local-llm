EXPENSE APPROVAL POLICY
========================
Effective Date: March 15, 2024
Version: 2.1
Classification: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE
----------
This policy defines the approval hierarchy and spending limits for all
business expenses, ensuring proper authorization and fiscal responsibility.

2. SCOPE
--------
Applies to all employees submitting expense reports, purchase requisitions,
or procurement requests through the ERP system.

3. APPROVAL THRESHOLDS
-----------------------
The following approval matrix applies to ALL expense categories:

  Amount Range (USD)       Required Approver(s)
  ─────────────────────    ──────────────────────────────────────
  ≤ $500                   Direct Supervisor
  $501 – $5,000            Department Manager
  $5,001 – $25,000         Department Manager + Finance Director
  $25,001 – $50,000        VP of Finance + CFO
  > $50,000                CFO + Board Approval

3.1  No expense may be self-approved regardless of the requester's seniority.
3.2  Split transactions intended to circumvent thresholds are strictly
     prohibited and subject to disciplinary action.

4. EXPENSE CATEGORIES
----------------------
4.1  TRAVEL: Must be pre-approved. Airfare economy class unless flight > 6 h.
     Hotel per-diem rates per company travel schedule. Meals capped at $75/day
     domestic, $100/day international.
4.2  OFFICE SUPPLIES: Standard items via approved vendor catalog only.
     Non-catalog items need manager approval.
4.3  SOFTWARE / IT: All software purchases must be vetted by IT Security for
     compliance and licensing.
4.4  CONSULTING / PROFESSIONAL SERVICES: Requires Statement of Work (SOW)
     signed by the department head and Legal review if > $10,000.
4.5  CAPITAL EXPENDITURES: Follow the CapEx policy; require Finance Director
     sign-off and budget confirmation.

5. SUBMISSION DEADLINES
------------------------
5.1  Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.
5.2  Reports older than 60 days require VP-level exception approval.
5.3  Fiscal year-end expenses must be submitted by December 20.

6. RECEIPT REQUIREMENTS
------------------------
6.1  Original receipts (or clear digital scans) required for all items > $25.
6.2  Missing receipts require a signed Missing Receipt Affidavit approved
     by the department manager.

7. AUDIT & COMPLIANCE
----------------------
7.1  Finance will audit 10% of submitted expense reports monthly.
7.2  Flagged violations are reported to the employee's VP and HR.

8. REVIEW CYCLE
----------------
Reviewed semi-annually. Next review: September 2024.
