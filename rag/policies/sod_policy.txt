SEGREGATION OF DUTIES (SoD) POLICY
====================================
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Version: 3.2
Classification: INTERNAL — CONFIDENTIAL

1. PURPOSE
----------
This policy establishes the Segregation of Duties framework to prevent fraud,
errors, and unauthorized activities in Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)
systems. SoD ensures that no single individual can control all phases of a
critical business transaction without independent oversight.

2. SCOPE
--------
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party users who
have access to the organization's ERP system (SAP, Oracle, or equivalent).

3. CORE PRINCIPLES
-------------------
3.1  No single person shall have the authority to both initiate AND approve
     the same financial transaction.
3.2  The following function pairs are considered conflicting and must NEVER
     be assigned to the same user:
       • Create Purchase Order  ↔  Approve Payment
       • Create Vendor Record   ↔  Process Invoice for that Vendor
       • Create Journal Entry   ↔  Post / Approve Journal Entry
       • Receive Goods          ↔  Approve Payment for those Goods
       • Manage User Access     ↔  Approve Financial Transactions
3.3  Emergency / break-glass access that temporarily combines conflicting
     duties must be logged, time-limited (max 24 hours), and reviewed by
     Internal Audit within 48 hours.

4. ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC)
-------------------------------------
4.1  Access to ERP transactions (T-codes) shall be granted strictly based on
     the employee's job function.
4.2  Role assignments must be reviewed quarterly by department managers and
     Internal Audit.
4.3  Any role change request must be approved by both the employee's manager
     AND the IT Security team.

5. MONITORING & DETECTION
--------------------------
5.1  Automated SoD conflict detection tools shall run daily against the
     access control matrix.
5.2  Detected violations must be reported to the Compliance Officer within
     24 hours.
5.3  Unresolved violations older than 30 days are escalated to the CFO.

6. CONSEQUENCES
----------------
Violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and
including termination, and may be referred to legal authorities if fraud
is suspected.

7. REVIEW CYCLE
----------------
This policy is reviewed annually by Internal Audit and the Chief Compliance
Officer. The next scheduled review is January 2025.
