VENDOR MANAGEMENT POLICY
=========================
Effective Date: February 1, 2024
Version: 1.8
Classification: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE
----------
This policy governs the end-to-end lifecycle of vendor relationships,
including onboarding, due diligence, performance monitoring, and
offboarding, to minimize supply chain risk and ensure regulatory compliance.

2. SCOPE
--------
Applies to all new and existing vendors, suppliers, contractors, and
service providers engaged by the organization.

3. VENDOR ONBOARDING
---------------------
3.1  All new vendor requests must be submitted through the ERP Vendor
     Master module by the requesting department.
3.2  Required documentation:
       • W-9 / W-8BEN (tax identification)
       • Certificate of Insurance (liability min $1M)
       • Bank account details (for ACH/wire setup)
       • Signed Vendor Code of Conduct
       • Compliance questionnaire (anti-bribery, data privacy)
3.3  Vendor master record creation and bank detail entry must be performed
     by DIFFERENT individuals (Segregation of Duties).
3.4  Procurement must verify that the vendor is not on any sanctions or
     debarment lists (OFAC, SAM.gov) before activation.
3.5  Approval chain for new vendors:
       Procurement Officer → Procurement Manager → Finance Director

4. VENDOR DUE DILIGENCE
-------------------------
4.1  Tier 1 vendors (annual spend > $100K): Full due diligence annually,
     including financial health check, compliance audit, and site visit.
4.2  Tier 2 vendors ($10K – $100K): Desk review and compliance
     questionnaire every 2 years.
4.3  Tier 3 vendors (< $10K): Self-certification at onboarding; spot
     checks as needed.

5. PERFORMANCE MONITORING
--------------------------
5.1  Quarterly scorecard reviews for Tier 1 vendors covering:
       • On-time delivery rate (target ≥ 95%)
       • Quality defect rate (target ≤ 2%)
       • Invoice accuracy (target ≥ 98%)
       • Responsiveness (issue resolution within SLA)
5.2  Vendors scoring below 70% for two consecutive quarters are placed
     on probation and must present a corrective action plan within 30 days.

6. VENDOR PAYMENT TERMS
-------------------------
6.1  Standard payment terms: Net 30 days from invoice receipt.
6.2  Early payment discounts (2/10 Net 30) may be negotiated by
     Procurement with Finance approval.
6.3  No advance payments without CFO and Legal approval.
6.4  Payments to vendors created within the last 90 days require
     additional review by AP Manager.

7. VENDOR OFFBOARDING
----------------------
7.1  Termination requires 30-day written notice unless contract specifies
     otherwise.
7.2  IT must revoke all system access within 24 hours of termination.
7.3  Outstanding invoices must be settled within 60 days of offboarding.

8. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
--------------------------
8.1  Employees must disclose any personal or financial relationship with
     a vendor or prospective vendor to the Compliance Officer.
8.2  Employees with disclosed conflicts are recused from vendor evaluation
     and approval decisions involving that vendor.

9. REVIEW CYCLE
----------------
Reviewed annually. Next review: February 2025.
